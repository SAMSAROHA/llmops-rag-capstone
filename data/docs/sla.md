# Aurora Cloud Service Level Agreement

## Uptime commitment

Aurora Cloud guarantees 99.95% monthly uptime for the Compute and Object Storage services on the Business and Enterprise plans. The Starter plan has a 99.5% uptime target but no financial guarantee.

Uptime is measured per calendar month across all regions where the customer has deployed resources. Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance does not count as downtime. Scheduled maintenance windows are Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, customers are eligible for service credits. Uptime between 99.0% and 99.95% earns a credit of 10% of the monthly bill for the affected service. Uptime between 95.0% and 99.0% earns a 25% credit. Uptime below 95.0% earns a 50% credit.

Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the month in which the incident occurred, by opening a billing ticket that includes the affected resource IDs. Credits are applied to the next invoice and are never paid out as cash. The total credit in a month cannot exceed 50% of that month's bill.

## Exclusions

The SLA does not cover outages caused by customer configuration changes, by exceeding documented quotas, or by features labelled Preview or Beta.
