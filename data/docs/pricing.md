# Aurora Cloud Plans and Pricing

## Plans

Aurora Cloud has three plans. The Starter plan costs $29 per month and includes 2 team seats, 50 GB of object storage and community support. The Business plan costs $249 per month and includes 15 team seats, 1 TB of object storage and email support with a 4-hour first response time during business hours. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing, unlimited seats, and 24/7 phone support with a 15-minute first response time for Severity 1 incidents.

## Usage charges

Compute is billed per second with a one-minute minimum. A standard vCPU costs $0.021 per hour and memory costs $0.0028 per GB-hour. Object storage beyond the plan allowance costs $0.018 per GB per month. Data egress is free for the first 100 GB each month and $0.07 per GB after that. Data ingress is always free.

## Billing

Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month for the previous month's usage and are due within 15 days. Annual prepayment gives a 15% discount on the plan fee but not on usage charges. Accounts more than 30 days overdue are suspended; data on suspended accounts is retained for 60 days before deletion.

## Free trial

New accounts get a 14-day free trial of the Business plan with $100 of usage credit. No credit card is required to start the trial.
